Delete your Strokeworks account

Strokeworks, the paddling app by Strokeworks

Your Strokeworks account is the Google account you signed in with for cloud backup. Deleting it deletes:

  • your cloud backup (every backed-up session, including routes, notes and photos, and your settings, boats, plans and profile details);
  • the sessions you shared with the Strokeworks team;
  • the account itself (your email address and Google account identifier).

Nothing is kept afterwards, except findings worked out from many paddlers' shared sessions that no longer contain any one person's session. Sessions stored on your phone aren't touched. Delete them in the app, or uninstall it.

In the app (immediate)

Open Strokeworks → Settings → Account and backup → Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email hello@strokeworks.app from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Strokeworks account". We'll delete everything listed above within 30 days and reply when it's done.

If you never signed in and only want shared sessions deleted, include the sharing ID shown in Settings.

See the privacy policy for more.
